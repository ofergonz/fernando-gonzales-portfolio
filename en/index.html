CASE 01 · PROCESS AUTOMATION
Implemented

Biweekly demurrage reporting automation

An automated workflow to consolidate container indicators and communicate recurring updates, replacing a manually assembled report.

Challenge

Tracking relied on consolidated information and a manually prepared summary. Data needed consistent standards and a clear distinction between completed cases and ongoing situations.

Solution

  • Structured a consolidated Excel table with consistent rules.
  • Defined calculations based on dates and free time.
  • Used Office Scripts to process updated data.
  • Configured Power Automate to run every 15 days and send an email summary.

Validation

Reviewed AI-assisted code, compared outputs against the spreadsheet and tested delivery to myself before adding recipients.

Key takeaway

Reliable automation requires explicit rules, exception handling and an understanding of how each tool updates and exposes data.

CASE 02 · ANALYSIS & DECISION-MAKING
Built and tested

Freight allocation simulator

A tool to compare cost allocation criteria and make each material's impact more transparent before choosing a method.

Challenge

Allocating freight only by weight can create disproportionate impacts on lower-value materials. The team needed a clear way to compare alternatives.

Solution

  • Compared value-based and weight-based allocation side by side.
  • Applied the lowest maximum individual impact criterion.
  • Added currency conversion, input validation and visual status indicators.
  • Organized instructions and process records, protecting formula cells.

Validation

Audited formulas with AI assistance, tested scenarios and refined the criterion with the team to reflect the agreed business rule.

Key takeaway

A technically correct calculation is only useful when its criteria reflect the business decision that needs to be made.

CASE 03 · DATA & RESPONSIBILITY
In progress

Workforce quota indicators calculator

A model in development to support the consolidation of disability and apprenticeship quota indicators across multiple legal entities.

Opportunity

The current analysis involves employee datasets and repetitive consolidation by company registration number. The goal is to reduce manual steps and make results easier to read.

Approach

  • Design a standardized structure for input and consolidation.
  • Use Power Query to organize data.
  • Create a summary of current count, required count and balance.
  • Develop with fictitious data only, without real personal records.

Safeguards

Applicable legal rules, percentages and classifications still require specialist validation before being incorporated into calculations.

Goal

Create a consolidated and traceable view while protecting privacy and making calculation assumptions explicit.
